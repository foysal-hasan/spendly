Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address, along with the expenses you log: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to run Spendly for you: to show your expenses, build category breakdowns and summaries, and keep your account secure. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and retained for as long as your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and associated expense data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads. We do not share your expense data with any other third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data removed, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
